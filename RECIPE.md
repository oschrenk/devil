
# Hario Switch, Water and Temp Managed

## Recipe/water-Temp-Managed

## Interests/coffee

A complex Switch recipe that does **three things at once**:

1. **Water mix** — blends tap and demineralized water in the kettle to control the mineral profile
2. **Temp mix** — actively cools the bed mid-brew with a room-temperature demineralized pour
3. **Coffee** — extracts a 15 g / 250 g brew on the Hario Switch

### Settings

- **Brewer**: Hario Switch
- **Server**: Hario 300 ml Beaker Server
- **Grinder**: 1Zpresso K-Ultra
- **Grind setting**: 7.8
- **Dose**: 15 g
- **Total water through bed**: 250 g
- **Brew ratio**: 1 : 16.7
- **Roast target**: Medium
- **Brew water composition**: ~50 % tap + ~50 % demineralized
- **Initial brew temp**: 92 °C
- **Mid-brew bed temp target**: ~75 °C
- **Total brew time**: ~3:30
- **Preheat kettle setting**: 96 °C (max usable at Guatemala City altitude before bubbling)

### Gear

- Hario Switch w/ Hario Neo Dripper
- Hario Hario V60 Paper filter, Size 02
- Codale Negotiator
- Hario 300 ml Beaker Server
- 1Zpresso K-Ultra grinder
- Fellow Staag Kettle
- Acaia Pearl
- **Beaker A** — medium (~150 ml): pre-measured demineralized water for the kettle
- **Beaker B** — small (~25 ml): room-temp demineralized water for the mid-brew cool-down
- Drinking cup

### Water Plan

**Preheat** — 300 ml total

- 200 ml: through Switch + filter into server
- 100 ml: into the cup
- Source: any water; kettle at 96 °C (boiling)

**Brew water in kettle** — ~263 g, heated to 92 °C

- 125 g tap water (intended brew portion)
- 25 g tap water (buffer for kettle dead volume + evaporation)
- 113 g demineralized water (from Beaker A)

**Mid-brew cooler** — 13 ml

- Pure demineralized water
- Room temperature (~20 °C)
- Held in Beaker B

> Total demin going through the bed ≈ 113 g (from the kettle) + 13 g (added mid-brew to cool the kettle) = ~126 g, against ~124 g tap (the 25 g buffer largely stays in the kettle).
> Cup ends up roughly **50/50 tap : demin**.

#### Optional: Single-Fill Kettle Optimization

To skip the kettle refill between preheat and brew:

1. Put **450 ml tap water** in the kettle from the start.
   Heat to 96 °C.
2. Pour off 300 ml for preheating (200 ml through Switch + filter into server, 100 ml into the cup).
3. The remaining 150 ml tap (= 125 g brew + 25 g buffer) stays in the kettle.
4. Add **113 g demineralized water** from Beaker A → kettle now holds the same ~263 g brew batch as the standard plan.
5. Reheat to 92 °C and proceed with the brew.

### Step-by-Step

#### 1. Prep

1. Grind 15 g coffee at K-Ultra **8.0**.
2. Place paper filter in Switch; sit Switch on top of Hario server.
3. Pre-measure into beakers:
   - **Beaker A**: 113 g demineralized water
   - **Beaker B**: 13 ml room-temp demineralized water
4. Drinking cup ready alongside the server.
5. Seat the filter into the switch

#### 2. Preheat (Kettle at 96 °C)

1. Boil ~300 ml in the kettle.
2. Pour ~200 ml through the Switch (filter in place), with closed switch and with negotiator seated into the Hario server.
   Rinses the filter and preheats Switch + server in one pass.
3. Pour ~100 ml into the drinking cup.
4. Just before brewing, discard preheat water from the server.
5. Just before serving, discard preheat water from the cup.

#### 3. Assemble Brew Water (Re-Set Kettle to 92 °C)

1. Refill kettle with **125 g tap water + 25 g buffer** (= 150 g tap).
2. Add **113 g demineralized water** from Beaker A.
3. Set kettle to 92 °C and bring to temperature.

#### 4. Brew (15 G Coffee · 250 G Total Through the Bed)

**0:00 — Bloom**

- Switch **closed** (down)
- Pour 50 g of 92 °C water for an immersion bloom

**~0:10 — Swirl**

- Switch still closed
- Brief swirl to settle the slurry

**0:30 — Pour 2 (50 g hot)**

- **Open switch first**, then pour 50 g
- Percolates through the bed

**1:00 — Pour 3 (75 g hot)**

- Switch still open
- Brings hot total to 175 g; this finishes the kettle pours

**~1:45 — Cold add + swirl**

- **Close switch** (down)
- Pour 13 ml cold demin from Beaker B into kettle
- This drops water temp in kettle to ~75 °C
- Pour 75 g
- Swirl
- Immersion phase begins

**2:30 — Drain**

- **Open switch** (up)
- Bed drains through

**~3:30 — Done**

- Last drips into server

#### 5. Serve

1. Swirl the server briefly to mix any layering.
2. Pour into the preheated cup.
   Drink.
